      How your progress is saved

      Your answers, flags, review schedule and test scores are saved in this browser on this device. You don't need an account.

      Or sign in, and it follows you

      Tap Sign in at the top (Face ID, your fingerprint on Android, your email, or Google). Your progress is then also saved to your account, and it shows up on every phone, tablet or computer where you sign in, the Home Screen app included.

      On an iPhone or iPad, study in the Home Screen app

      • In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
      • Safari can clear a site's saved data after a week of using Safari without opening that site. The Home Screen app doesn't have that limit, so your progress is safer there.
      • The Home Screen app keeps its own copy, separate from Safari. Pick one and study there.

      On Android

      In Chrome, tap the menu (⋮), then Install app or Add to Home screen. The installed app and Chrome share the same progress.

      Moving your progress without an account

      1. Where you've been studying, tap Export my progress at the bottom of the page and save the file (Files or iCloud Drive).
      2. In the Home Screen app or on the other device, tap Import progress and pick that file.

      That brings everything, including your class if you joined one.

      Your sign-in code

      If you joined a class, the sign-in code (Learn → Your questions) moves only your class and your questions to another device. It does not bring your study progress. Sign in, or use Export and Import, for that.

      Export a copy now and then as a backup. Clearing Safari's history and website data also wipes it.
