Privacy

Balloon Written Prep · last updated 23 October 2026

Balloon Written Prep is a free study app for the FAA Private and Commercial balloon written tests. It is open source, and it has no ads, no trackers and no analytics.

Without an account

Your answers, flags, review schedule and test scores are saved only in your browser on your device. Nothing about your studying is sent anywhere, unless you send a practice test score to your instructor (below).

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. It saves a copy of your study progress to your account, so it shows up on every device where you sign in. We keep:

  • Your study progress: which practice questions you answered and how, your flags, review schedule, practice test scores and test date.
  • How you sign in, and nothing more:
    • Face ID (passkey): the passkey's public key. The private key never leaves your device, and we never see your face or fingerprint.
    • Email code: a one-way scrambled form (a salted hash) of your email address, so we can recognise it next time. The address itself is not stored. We use it once, to send the code.
    • Google: your Google account number and the scrambled form of your email. We ask Google only for your email address, never your name, contacts, files or anything else.

We do not sell, share or use this for anything except keeping your progress in sync. Rate limits use a scrambled form of your network address that cannot be turned back into the address, and those records are deleted after two days.

Instructors and classes

If you join an instructor's class, your instructor sees your name and the questions you send them.

Your instructor sees a practice test score only when you send it. After a practice test, the results page asks whether to send that score; nothing goes unless you tap Send to my instructor, or you have turned on Always send my practice test scores for that class (you can turn it off there any time). What is sent for each test:

  • which test it was (Private or Commercial), how many questions, how many you got right, the percentage and whether it passed;
  • the time you spent on it, and when you finished it;
  • how many you got right in each subject area;
  • how many of the test's questions you had flagged in each subject area (a count, not which questions).

Your answers, the questions themselves, your flags on other questions and the rest of your study history are never sent. Leaving the class, or being removed from it by your instructor, deletes every score you sent. This works the same whether or not you sign in.

Deleting your account

In the app, tap your account at the top, then Delete my account and saved progress. That deletes the account and everything saved in it straight away. The progress on your device stays until you reset it there. If your phone still lists a passkey for this app, you can delete it in the Passwords app.

Where it is kept

Accounts and progress are stored on Cloudflare (Workers and D1). Codes are emailed from the app's own Gmail account. Google sign-in follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Questions

The app's code is public: github.com/brianfliesballoons/balloon-prep. You can open an issue there with any privacy question.
